🔗 How the Chain Works
Student 1 — Says only their own info
"My name is Ana. I am 52 years old. I live in Guadalajara. I am a teacher."
Student 2 — Repeats Student 1 + adds their own
"She is Ana. She is 52. She is from Guadalajara. She is a teacher."
"My name is Carlos. I am 60. I am from Monterrey. I am a doctor."
Student 3 — Repeats 1 + 2 + adds their own
"She is Ana... He is Carlos..."
"My name is Rosa. I am 58. I am from CDMX. I am a nurse."
Last student — Repeats everyone + adds their own! 🏆
Repeats all previous students and then says their own info last. The hardest spot!
