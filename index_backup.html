SLANGS Reaction Slangs — Presentation
Book Page —
1
Really?
Use when something surprises you or you want to confirm
2
That's cool!
Use when something impresses you or sounds interesting
3
No way!
Use when something is hard to believe or very surprising
4
Me too!
Use when you share the same feeling or experience
5
Awesome!
Use when something is great, exciting or impressive
